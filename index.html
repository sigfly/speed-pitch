How it works & troubleshooting

Phone sensors measure acceleration, not absolute speed. This instrument estimates short-term movement speed and gently forgets old motion to prevent drift. Smooth swings work best. Constant-speed travel cannot be reliably detected. If silent, allow motion access, turn up media volume and use HTTPS in your phone browser. Hold the phone securely, with room to move.
